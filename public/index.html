Why BitMask

Private by architecture.

Every interaction is designed to keep control close to you.

End-to-End Encrypted

ECDH key exchange + AES-256-GCM. The server never sees your messages.

Self-Destructing

Messages vanish after 5 seconds to 24 hours. You choose the timer.

Group Rooms

Encrypted rooms for 3+ peers. Share a 6-char code and everyone's in.

Zero Knowledge

No accounts. No logs. No phone numbers. Identity dies when you close the tab.

Media that behaves

Send images by attach, paste, or drop. Encrypted previews stay local and clean themselves up.
